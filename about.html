About AZoom

Simple, honest electric car rental. Book online, collect from one of our two locations, return to any of our car parks.

Our story

AZoom Car Rental was started with one idea: renting an electric car should take minutes, not paperwork. You choose a car and pay online, our team hands you the keys at your chosen location, and when you're done you simply leave the car at one of our designated car parks. We inspect every returned car and charge only for damage we actually find, so your final bill is always clear and fair.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I reserve a car?

    Log in, go to the Cars page and choose an electric car. Then pick your pick-up location, your pick-up and return dates, and enter your credit card. You'll get a booking ID straight away.

    What do I bring when I collect the car?

    Just your booking ID. Show it to AZoom staff at your chosen pick-up location and they will hand over the car.

    Where do I return the car?

    At any of our designated car parks listed above. Choose the one you used on the My booking page.

    How are damages charged?

    Open My booking, enter your booking ID, and once the inspection is done you'll see an itemised bill and a Pay button.
